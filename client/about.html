Our Story

Breaking the walls between departments at Banasthali Vidyapith.

In a campus as big as ours, we are often stuck in departmental silos. Whether it's a semester break or a sudden emergency, finding a cab partner usually means spamming your course WhatsApp group and hoping for a miracle.

Travio is a centralized hub for all Banasthalians. A B.Tech student can now find a Law or MBA student heading to the airport or Newai station. No more scrolling through 100+ "Anyone going to Delhi?" messages. Just search, connect, and travel safely.

Why Use Travio?

Cross-Campus Reach: Connect with girls from every hostel and department.
Smart Filtering: Search by date and destination specifically for break dates.
Cost Efficient: Share cab fares and save money for your next treat!
